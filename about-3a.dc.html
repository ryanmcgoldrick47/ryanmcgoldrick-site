about

industrial designer based in wollongong, australia, working across object, furniture, product and lighting.

a focus in honest, products that look to function, material use and playful spirit for inspiration.

ryan mcgoldrick is a wollongong based industrial designer working across object, furniture, lighting and product.

alongside his own studio product and project pieces exploring both modern and traditional manufacturing methods, ryan has developed furniture, consumer electronics and robotics for leading international brands.

trained in industrial design at the university of new south wales (sydney, australia) with exchange at the bauhaus university in weimar, ryan began his interests in products and making from an early age, completing formal qualifications in metal fabrication and welding at 14. ryan prototypes and makes much of his work by hand and working with local makers.

his work has been exhibited at melbourne design week, milan design week, sculptures by the sea and recognised in awards from core77 and the good design awards australia. for commissions and collaborations, please get in touch with the studio via e-mail or instagram.
