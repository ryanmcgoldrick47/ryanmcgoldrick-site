ryan mcgoldrick, wollongong

Ryan McGoldrick is a Wollongong based industrial designer working across object, furniture, lighting and product. The work begins with an honest look at material and function, and always leaves room for a little play.

Alongside his own studio pieces, including folded brass lamps, extruded aluminium seating and coasters pressed from malt rootlets, Ryan has designed furniture, audio hardware and robotics for leading Australian companies including King Living, RØDE and Hullbot.

His work has been exhibited at Milan Design Week and Sculptures by the Sea, and recognised by Core77 and the Good Design Awards Australia. For commissions and collaborations, get in touch via email or Instagram.
